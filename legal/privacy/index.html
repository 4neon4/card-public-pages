KardMego Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 September 2026

1. Plain-language summary

KardMego is a loyalty-card and shopping organiser. We process only the data needed for the features you choose, security and legal compliance. The current app does not include advertising, cross-app tracking or general product-analytics SDKs.

In the current implementation:

  • account, card, barcode, family, sharing, shopping-list and shopping-history records can be synchronised through our Supabase backend;
  • card-front and receipt photos remain local to your device; when you explicitly add a photo to an eligible cloud-synchronised shopping-list item, the processed item photo is stored in private cloud storage so currently authorised list participants can view it;
  • the item-photo source is processed on the device, stripped of embedded EXIF/GPS metadata, converted to WebP and limited to 768 × 768 pixels and 256,000 bytes before upload;
  • receipt scanning/text recognition and image cropping are performed on the device;
  • foreground location can be converted into country, city and an approximate grid area for store-country and ASK/GO functions; KardMego does not store raw latitude and longitude in its database;
  • permissions can be refused or revoked, but the related feature may stop working; and
  • you can request account deletion in the app.

This summary does not replace the full notice below.

3. Data we process

3.1 Account and authentication data

When you create or use an account, we process your email address, Supabase user identifier, authentication/session information, email-confirmation and password-reset status, account timestamps and basic profile settings. Password verification is handled by the authentication provider; KardMego should not log or store your plaintext password.

3.2 Guest identity data

If guest mode is available, the device creates a guest card identifier and secret. A hash of the secret, display name, guest identifier, family relationships and last-seen information can be stored in the backend. The original guest secret is kept in secure device storage.

3.3 Card and barcode data

We process information you add to a card, such as store name/key, barcode or QR value and type, notes, favourite status, owner, access mode, family-sharing setting, and synchronisation timestamps. A card-front photo/logo is currently stored only in the app's local files. A barcode value can identify or provide access to a third-party loyalty account, so it should be treated as sensitive even though it is not normally a special category of data under the GDPR.

3.4 Family and sharing data

We process family membership and invitation/claim records, share requests, request store and status, shared-access owner and recipient identifiers, access duration and expiry, card acknowledgements/thanks, and information required to display a shared card. Depending on your settings, a recipient can see the store name, barcode/QR value and type, notes, and sharing status.

3.5 Shopping and receipt records

We process shopping-list identifiers, card/store association, product names, categories, quantity/unit text, completion status and time, sorting information, archive entries, finish time, notes and receipt-total text. Receipt photos remain local to the device. If you explicitly add an item photo to an eligible cloud-synchronised list, the app processes the image on the device, strips embedded EXIF/GPS metadata, converts it to WebP and limits it to 768 × 768 pixels and 256,000 bytes. The original file is not uploaded by this feature. Supabase stores access-controlled photo metadata and the processed bytes are stored in a private Cloudflare R2 bucket. The operating system may temporarily process images when you use its camera, picker or share interface.

3.6 Location and store-country data

When you allow foreground location, the device may obtain coordinates from the operating system and reverse-geocode them. KardMego stores or transmits the selected/detected country code, a normalised city key, an approximate grid-area key (currently based on roughly 0.02-degree cells), location-update time and location source. Raw latitude and longitude are not stored in the KardMego database by the current implementation.

Approximate location data is used for the store-country setting and ASK/GO request matching. Location is obtained only while using the relevant foreground function; the current app does not request background location.

3.7 Device, permission and notification data

We process operating-system permission state and app preferences on the device. If push notifications are enabled and supported, a device push token, platform and account association may be stored so a Service notification can be delivered. Android currently also uses local notifications for certain events. Apple, Google and device manufacturers independently process data needed to provide their platforms and push services.

3.8 Local storage and diagnostics

The app uses encrypted/secure device storage for sessions, legal acceptance, guest credentials, language/theme/country preferences and a local location snapshot, and local files/database storage for cards, images, shopping media and caches. Production infrastructure may create security, access and error logs containing timestamps, network/device metadata, request identifiers and limited account identifiers. We use these records to operate, secure and troubleshoot the Service.

3.9 Support communications

If you contact us, we process your name/contact details, message, attachments, account or device details you provide, and our response history. Do not send passwords, unnecessary full barcode values, medical information or other sensitive content.

4. Where data comes from

We receive data:

  • directly from you when you create an account, add a card or list, share access, explicitly select an image for local use or upload an item photo, or contact support;
  • from your device and operating system when you grant permission or use a feature;
  • from other users when they invite you, add you to a family, request a card, share a card with you or update a shared list; and
  • from service providers that operate authentication, hosting, database, email, notifications and security functions.

If you provide another person's data, you must have authority to do so and should ensure the person receives appropriate information about the sharing.

6. How sharing works and who receives data

6.1 Other KardMego users

Data is disclosed to family members, request participants and recipients only according to the feature and access you select. Shared card access may expose store name, barcode/QR value/type, notes and timing/status. Participants who currently have access to a shared shopping list can see and edit relevant list information and view its active item photos. Photo access is checked by the server for each temporary download grant; removing family/list access blocks new server grants after the change is synchronised. Requesters and card owners may see request status and limited profile/display information.

6.2 Processors and platform providers

We use providers to operate the Service, including providers in the following categories:

  • Supabase for authentication, database records, access control, Edge Functions and related infrastructure;
  • Cloudflare Workers and private R2 object storage for processed shopping-item photo upload, delivery and cleanup;
  • email-delivery infrastructure for confirmation and password-reset messages;
  • Apple and Google for app distribution, operating-system services, notifications, permissions and optional device backup;
  • website hosting and domain providers;
  • support, security, logging or monitoring providers, if enabled.

Processors may use data only under our instructions and appropriate contractual safeguards, except where a provider acts independently under law or its own platform terms.

6.3 Authorities and legal recipients

6.4 Business transfer

If the Service or operator is reorganised, merged or transferred, relevant data may be transferred with appropriate notice, confidentiality and data-protection safeguards. We do not sell personal data.

8. Retention

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the stated purpose, legal obligations, security and claims.

In particular:

  • active account, card and current shopping data: while the account/feature is active, then deleted after an account or item deletion request, subject to short operational and backup periods;
  • local card-front and receipt images: until you delete the image/card/history, clear app data, uninstall the app or delete the account using a flow that removes local files;
  • active cloud-synchronised item photos: while the relevant item remains active and the photo is needed; after replacement/removal or the end/deletion of the item or list, user access ends immediately and the old private R2 object is scheduled for permanent deletion within 24 hours;
  • unfinished item-photo uploads: never shown as active and scheduled for permanent deletion no later than 24 hours after they become stale or are cancelled;
  • item-photo offline cache: stored in app-private device files while the photo remains available; removed after a successful snapshot or access check confirms replacement, removal or loss of access, during supported account cleanup, when app data is cleared, or when the app is uninstalled. A device that remains offline may retain its existing cache until it reconnects or its app data is removed;
  • completed shopping archives: automated cleanup is configured to delete entries older than 90 days and retain no more than 30 newest entries per card;
  • share requests: pending requests expire after 7 days; unreferenced requests older than 90 days are deleted;
  • temporary shared access: disabled when it expires, with access records retained only as needed for security and legal claims;
  • guest profiles: until deletion or account linkage;
  • push tokens: until notifications are disabled, the token becomes invalid or the account is deleted;
  • legal acceptance records: for as long as necessary to demonstrate acceptance, comply with law and establish or defend claims;
  • support and security logs: only for as long as needed to provide support, protect the Service and meet legal obligations; and
  • backups: if data remains in protected backups after deletion, it is isolated from ordinary use and removed through the applicable backup cycle.

9. Account and data deletion

Account holders can request deletion in the app and at https://kardmego.app/legal/delete-account. We may verify identity to prevent unauthorised deletion. The deletion function removes the authentication account and associated backend records, including device tokens, shopping data, profile, related account records and the guest profile claimed by that account. After a successful response, the app attempts to remove identified local account caches, media, guest credentials, local legal cache, language/theme/country/location preferences and the local session. If that local cleanup is interrupted or device storage is unavailable, some local data may remain until cleanup succeeds or you clear the app data or uninstall the app. We do not describe this process as crash-atomic or as an absolute local-erasure guarantee.

Deletion cannot remove:

  • information another user independently copied or retained outside KardMego;
  • records we must keep temporarily to comply with law, resolve disputes, prevent fraud or demonstrate a legal transaction;
  • data in encrypted backups until the normal backup cycle expires, provided it is isolated from ordinary use; or
  • data controlled independently by a retailer, Apple, Google or another third party.

Deleting a single card, photo or shopping record should remove it from active app storage according to the feature. Contact us if the in-app control is unavailable.
